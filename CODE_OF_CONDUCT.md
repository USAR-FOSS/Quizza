# Code of Conduct

Quizza welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be
respectful, explain decisions, accept constructive feedback, and help newcomers.
Critique ideas and code without attacking people.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.
Do not spam issues, pressure volunteers, or retaliate against reports.

This policy covers project repositories, reviews, discussions, event spaces,
and communications where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to siddharthb@fossunited.org with relevant context and
immediate safety concerns. Maintainers will limit sharing to what is needed to
investigate and respond. If a report concerns a maintainer, contact another
event organizer directly.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request corrections, issue warnings,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should reflect
severity, repetition, and affected people's safety. Anyone affected by an
enforcement decision may request a review by email.
